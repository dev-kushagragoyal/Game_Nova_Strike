# 🚀 NOVA STRIKE

### Survive the Swarm. Become the Legend.

**NOVA STRIKE** is a futuristic arcade-style space shooter built entirely with **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript**.

Take control of your spacecraft, survive waves of hostile enemies, build devastating combos, collect powerful upgrades, and fight massive boss encounters as the difficulty increases.

---

## 🎮 Game Features

### ⚡ Fast-Paced Combat

Move your ship across the battlefield and destroy incoming enemies before they overwhelm you.

### 👾 Multiple Enemy Types

Fight different enemy classes, each with their own movement patterns, health, damage, and behavior.

* **Drone** — Fast and lightweight
* **Striker** — Aggressive attacking enemy
* **Cruiser** — Heavier combat unit
* **Tank** — High-health enemy

### 👹 Boss Battles

Every **5 levels**, the battlefield introduces a powerful boss encounter:

> ⚠️ VOID REAPER ⚠️

Boss battles feature a dedicated health bar and significantly increase the challenge.

### 🔥 Combo System

Destroy enemies consecutively to increase your combo multiplier.

Higher combos mean higher scores.

Don't let the combo expire!

### 💥 Power-Ups

Collect power-ups during gameplay to gain temporary advantages:

| Power-Up       | Effect                          |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 🔥 Rapid Fire  | Increases firing speed          |
| 🛡️ Shield     | Protects the player from damage |
| ✨ Double Score | Multiplies earned score         |

### 🌌 Procedural Visuals

The game creates its futuristic atmosphere using Canvas-based procedural effects, including:

* Animated star fields
* Space/nebulas effects
* Shooting stars
* Particle explosions
* Energy trails
* Screen shake
* Damage effects
* Neon-style visual effects

### 🔊 Web Audio

NOVA STRIKE uses the **Web Audio API** to generate game sounds dynamically without requiring external audio files.

This includes:

* Weapon firing
* Explosions
* Power-up collection
* Damage effects
* Boss events
* Game-over effects

### 📱 Responsive Controls

The game supports both desktop and mobile gameplay.

**Desktop**

```text
A / ←       Move Left
D / →       Move Right
SPACE       Fire
P           Pause
ENTER       Launch
```

**Mobile**

Use the on-screen:

```text
←    FIRE    →
```

controls.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
2. Click **LAUNCH MISSION**.
3. Move your spacecraft using `A/D` or the arrow keys.
4. Hold `SPACE` to fire.
5. Destroy enemies and build your combo.
6. Collect power-ups.
7. Survive increasingly difficult levels.
8. Defeat the **VOID REAPER** during boss levels.
9. Beat your highest score.

---

## 🏆 High Score System

Your best score is automatically saved using browser **LocalStorage**.

This means your high score remains available even after refreshing or reopening the game in the same browser.

## 🎯 Project Goals

NOVA STRIKE was designed to demonstrate how a complete browser game can be created using native web technologies without relying on a game engine.

The project combines:

* Game-loop architecture
* Real-time animation
* Canvas rendering
* Collision detection
* Enemy AI
* Procedural effects
* Audio synthesis
* Responsive UI
* State management
* Browser storage

into one playable experience.
